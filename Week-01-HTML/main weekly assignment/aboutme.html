ABOUT ME 😀



1. Biography

Bezia Precious is a third-year Computer Engineering student specializing in Software Engineering at the University of Buea. His journey in technology has been driven by curiosity, creativity, and a desire to understand how technology can be used to solve real-world problems. Over the years, he has developed experience beyond the classroom through academic projects, tutoring, educational programs, graphic design, and coordination of student-focused initiatives. He has been involved in educational activities that have reached more than 3,000 students, giving him valuable experience in leadership, communication, teamwork, and organization. As a software engineering student, he continues to develop his knowledge in programming, system design, databases, and software development. He is particularly interested in combining technical knowledge with creativity and business thinking to develop useful and sustainable technology solutions. He considers continuous learning, adaptability, discipline, and practical experience essential to becoming a competent technology professional.

Career Goals

My long-term goal is to become a highly skilled software engineer capable of designing, developing, and managing reliable technology solutions that solve meaningful real-world problems. I aim to build strong expertise through continuous learning, practical experience, and exposure to professional software development environments. In the future, I would also like to combine my technical expertise with entrepreneurship and leadership to create technology-driven products and businesses that can have a meaningful impact, particularly within Africa. Ultimately, I want my career to go beyond simply writing code. I want to understand problems deeply, design effective solutions, work with talented teams, and contribute to the development of technology that creates measurable value.



Why I Joined the Internship

I joined this internship because I believe that classroom knowledge alone is not enough to become a competent software engineer. I want to gain firsthand exposure to how software is designed, developed, tested, deployed, and maintained in a professional environment. The internship provides me with an opportunity to bridge the gap between academic theory and industry practice, work on real-world problems, collaborate with professionals, and understand the standards and expectations of the technology industry. I also joined because I want to challenge myself, identify areas where I need to improve, learn from experienced professionals, and develop the confidence and practical skills required for my future career. Most importantly, I see this internship as an opportunity to grow—not only as a software engineering student, but also as a future technology professional and problem-solver.
