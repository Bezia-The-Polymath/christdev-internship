Project Name: MoneyTracker

About the Project

MoneyTracker is an expense-tracking website designed for individuals who want to monitor and manage their expenditures.

The project provides users with a simple way to record their expenses and understand their spending habits. It organizes expense information so that users can easily review their financial activities.


Why MoneyTracker?

Managing personal expenses can become difficult when spending is not properly recorded. Small purchases can add up over time, making it difficult to know how much money has been spent.

MoneyTracker aims to make expense tracking easier by allowing users to record their expenditures and view their total expenses.


What Can You Track?

MoneyTracker can be used to record expenses such as:

  • Food and groceries
  • Transportation
  • Bills
  • Education
  • Shopping
  • Entertainment
  • Healthcare
  • Other personal expenses

Reporting Periods

Users can review their expenses based on different periods:

  • Daily
  • Weekly
  • Monthly
  • Yearly
  • Custom period

Our Goal

The goal of MoneyTracker is to provide a simple and organized way for individuals to monitor their spending and make better-informed financial decisions.


MoneyTracker Terms

Expense

Money spent on a product, service, or other personal need.

Total Expense

The combined amount of all recorded expenses.

Expense Report

A summary of expenses recorded during a selected period.

Custom Period

A user-selected period between a specific start date and end date.
