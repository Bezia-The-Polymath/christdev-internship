Take Control of Your Money

Welcome to MoneyTracker, a simple and convenient expense-tracking platform designed to help individuals monitor their spending and better understand where their money goes.

With MoneyTracker, you can record your daily expenses, calculate your total spending, and generate expense reports for different periods.


What Does MoneyTracker Do?

MoneyTracker helps you keep track of your expenditures in an organized way. Instead of trying to remember where your money went, you can record each expense and review your spending whenever you need to.

Main Features

  • Expense Tracking — Record and organize your daily expenses.
  • Total Expense Calculation — See how much you have spent.
  • Daily Reports — Review your expenses for a specific day.
  • Weekly Reports — Monitor your spending throughout the week.
  • Monthly Reports — Understand your monthly spending habits.
  • Yearly Reports — Review your expenses over an entire year.
  • Custom Period Reports — Select a specific period and view your expenses.
