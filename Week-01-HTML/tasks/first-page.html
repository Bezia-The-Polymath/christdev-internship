Why I Decided to Learn Software Development

There are several reasons that made me venture into the field but below are the main two:

Reason 1: Its Applicability

Software Development can be applied to all human activities, giving me a wide net to fish in and not just limited to a specific domain.
It can automate tasks, create websites, and even build mobile applications. This versatility is what drew me to the field in the first place.

Reason 2: Problem-Solving

Software development is fundamentally about solving problems.
I enjoy the challenge of breaking down complex issues into manageable components and creating solutions that can make a real difference in people's lives.
