My Journey into Software Development

This is a simple article about my journey into Software Development

.

My Journey into Software Development1 started in high school, with a topic that completely changed the way I looked at computers: algorithms. At first, an algorithm seemed like just another topic to study. But as I began to understand it, I became fascinated by the idea that almost anything could be represented as a series of clear, step-by-step instructions. That realization really caught my attention. I discovered that if a process could be broken down into steps, it could be expressed as an algorithm. Something as simple as preparing a meal could have an algorithm: gather the ingredients, prepare them, follow the cooking steps, and arrive at the final result1 meal. It made me realize that algorithms weren't just abstract concepts in a computer science textbook—they were a way of representing how we solve problems in the real world.

I also began looking at everyday technology differently. For example, services like MTN Mobile Money made me think about the processes happening behind the scenes. When you send money, check your balance, or make a payment, there is a sequence of instructions and decisions that allows the system to produce the expected result. That was the point where my interest in computer science became much deeper. I started to see software not simply as something that runs on a computer, but as a way of turning ideas, processes, and solutions into something that can actually work. That curiosity eventually influenced one of the biggest decisions I made about my education: choosing to pursue Software Engineering at the university. Today, my journey has gone far beyond that first introduction to algorithms. I have learned about programming, databases, software design, computer networks, artificial intelligence, and many other areas of computing. But whenever I think about where it all started, I go back to that moment in high school when I realized that a process could be transformed into an algorithm. That simple idea sparked a curiosity that has continued to grow. My journey into software development2 started with algorithms—and it continues with my desire to use technology to turn ideas and problems into real solutions.
